Measuring performance

How to actually track what these links raise.

01 — GA4

Traffic acquisition by campaign

Since GA4 is already installed, UTM parameters are picked up automatically. Use Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, and add utm_campaign / utm_source / utm_medium as secondary dimensions to see sessions and conversions per link.

02 — Your donation platform

Join revenue to campaign

If your donation platform lets you name or tag campaigns/forms, keep that name identical to utm_campaign. That shared name is what lets you join your platform's revenue export to GA4 sessions — giving you revenue per link, not just clicks.

03 — The Ledger

One log, one source of truth

Export this ledger to CSV regularly and keep it alongside your donation platform and GA4 exports. That running log becomes the master list you cross-reference when reporting results.
