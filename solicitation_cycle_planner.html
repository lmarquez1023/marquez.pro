Step 01

Pull your gift history.

The planner reads one row per gift. Run a gift query or report on your portfolio and export it to CSV or Excel.

How much data to provide
  • Time spanEvery gift from the first day of the fiscal year five years ago through today. Three full fiscal years is the minimum; five lets the planner spot each donor's usual giving month and trend.
  • DonorsOne portfolio at a time works best, typically 75 to 250 donors. A team file works too if it includes a solicitor column; you can plan one gift officer at a time.
  • RowsOne row per gift, not one row per donor. Files up to about 100,000 rows run comfortably.
  • Gift typesInclude cash, pledge payments, stock, and recurring gift payments. Leave out pledge commitments (their payments are already counted), soft credits, and matching gifts credited to a company.
Columns to include
ColumnUsed for
Donor IDRequiredGroups gifts by donor. Constituent ID, account ID, or any unique donor number.
Gift dateRequiredPlaces each gift in a fiscal year and month. MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
Gift amountRequiredBuilds yearly totals, trends, and the ask.
Donor nameRecommendedMakes the plan readable. Without it, the plan shows IDs.
Rated capacityOptionalA dollar amount or range from wealth screening, such as "$25,000 - $49,999." Keeps asks in line with capacity and flags stretch opportunities.
SolicitorOptionalLets you plan one gift officer's portfolio from a team file.
Step-by-step for your database
Raiser's Edge (database view)
  1. Go to Query, then New Query, and choose a Gift query (dynamic).
  2. Criteria: Gift Date between and today. Gift Type one of Cash, Pay-Cash, Stock/Property, Stock/Property (Sold), Recurring Gift Pay-Cash.
  3. Portfolio criteria: Constituent Information, Solicitors, Name equals the gift officer (skip this to pull the whole team). Add Deceased equals No.
  4. Output: Constituent ID, Name, Gift Date, Gift Amount. Optional: your capacity rating from Prospect, Ratings and Solicitors, Name.
  5. Run the query and export it to CSV.
Raiser's Edge NXT (web view)
  1. Open Lists, then Gifts.
  2. Filter Gift date on or after and Gift type to cash, pledge payments, stock, and recurring gift payments.
  3. Filter Fundraiser to the gift officer, or leave it open for the whole team.
  4. Columns: Constituent lookup ID, Constituent name, Gift date, Gift amount, and optionally Fundraiser and a capacity rating.
  5. Export the list to CSV.
Salesforce, Bloomerang, Virtuous, or another CRM
  1. Build a gift, donation, or Opportunity report with received or Closed Won gifts only.
  2. Date filter: gift or close date on or after .
  3. Filter to the portfolio owner or relationship manager.
  4. Columns: Account or constituent ID, Account name, Gift or close date, Amount, and optionally Owner and a capacity field.
  5. Export as CSV, details only, no subtotals.
