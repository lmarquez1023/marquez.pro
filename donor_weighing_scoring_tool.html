How the scoring works

1. Roll up per donor. Every donor's raw gifts are collapsed into three numbers: most recent gift date (all-time), gift count in the lookback window, and total giving in the lookback window.

2. Score each factor 1–5. Recency, Frequency, and Monetary are each converted to a 1–5 score using the band tables in Assumptions, so they're all on the same scale.

3. Blend into one weighted score. Weighted Score = (Recency × Recency Weight) + (Frequency × Frequency Weight) + (Monetary × Monetary Weight), scaled to 0–100.

4. Assign tier & owner. The weighted score is checked against the Tier Cutoffs table, highest cutoff first, to assign a tier and an owner.

Recency uses full history, not the window. A donor's most recent gift date is never limited by the lookback window, so a long-lapsed donor still shows their true last gift date instead of looking like they never gave at all.
